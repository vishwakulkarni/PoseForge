> "Compared to PuLID-v1, better compatibility, editability, facial naturalness, and similarity."

## Notes

Modern identity-adapter evidence with unusually candid subgroup and precision limitations. The authors report about a five-point ID-similarity improvement for FLUX v0.9.1, but this is not independent and not comparable to other repos' metrics. FLUX.1-dev licensing applies.
