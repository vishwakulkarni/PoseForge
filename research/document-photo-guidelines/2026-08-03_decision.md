# Document photo implementation decision

PoseForge now distinguishes `retrievedOn` from the official source's own revision/effective date. When an official page does not state an update date, the UI says so rather than substituting the retrieval date.

The hypotheses were supported with one qualification: U.S. passport and visa geometry can share a 600 × 600 / 2 × 2 profile, but remain separate presets with separate official sources. India's new passport format is portrait 35 × 45 mm / 630 × 810 pixels, while its visa/e-Visa and OCI uploads are square with different byte and background limits.

Adversarial review identified the largest product risk: multiple authorities require an untouched current likeness, India passport guidance prohibits computer alteration, and OCI prohibits retouching. Therefore local crop/resize/format processing is the default. AI background assistance remains explicitly experimental and never carries a compliance guarantee.

The current source corpus is primary-government heavy by design because these are application rules, not interpretive claims. Where mission and central Indian pages conflict, the newer ICAO passport guidance and stricter central OCI portal limits win, and the conflict is recorded.
