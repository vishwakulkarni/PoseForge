# India regular visa photo guidance

- URL: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/visa/instruction.html
- Image specification: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/visa/VSS_IMAGE.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-03
- Editorial update date: not stated

The official upload target is square JPEG, 350–1000 pixels and 10–300 KB. Guidance calls for full-face front view, eyes open, centered head, plain light/white background, and no shadows or border.
