# India e-Visa photo guidance

- URL: https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/tvoa.html
- Retrieved: 2026-08-03
- Page-stated update: May 16, 2019

The official page specifies a square JPEG, 10 KB–1 MB, with full face, eyes open, no spectacles, centered full head, plain light/white background, and no shadow or border.
