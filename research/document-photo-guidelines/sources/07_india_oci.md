# India OCI photo guidance

- URL: https://ociservices.gov.in/onlineOCI/faq
- Photo PDF: https://ociservices.gov.in/Photo-Spec-FINAL.pdf
- Retrieved: 2026-08-03
- FAQ editorial update date: not stated

The central portal requires square JPEG/JPG, 200–900 pixels and no more than 200 KB. The face target is approximately 80%, with full face and shoulders centered on a plain light-colored background that is not white. The linked specification prohibits retouching, enhancement, and softening.
