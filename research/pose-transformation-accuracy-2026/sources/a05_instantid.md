## Verbatim quotes

> "InstantID is a new state-of-the-art tuning-free method to achieve ID-Preserving generation with only single image, supporting various downstream tasks."

> "For higher similarity, increase the weight of controlnet_conditioning_scale (IdentityNet) and ip_adapter_scale (Adapter)."

> "For higher text control ability, decrease ip_adapter_scale."

> "We have not supported multi-person yet, only use the largest face as reference facial landmarks."

> "However, both manual-downloading and auto-downloading face models from insightface are for non-commercial research purposes only"

> "Our released checkpoints are also for research purposes only."

## Notes

Documents the central fidelity-versus-editability tradeoff and a multi-person limitation. It is unsuitable as-is for a commercial multi-character product despite Apache-licensed code.
