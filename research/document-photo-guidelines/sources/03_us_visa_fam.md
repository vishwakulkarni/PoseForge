# U.S. visa photo standards

- URL: https://fam.state.gov/FAM/09FAM/09FAM030306.html#M303_6_2_A_1
- Retrieved: 2026-08-03
- Section revision: CT:VISA-2036; 07-29-2024

Evidence: “Visa applicants must provide with their applications un-mounted, full-face photographs ... taken within the past six months”; “Glossy, untouched, un-mounted, on a white or off-white background”; and hard copy “should measure 2 inches square (roughly 50 mm square).”
