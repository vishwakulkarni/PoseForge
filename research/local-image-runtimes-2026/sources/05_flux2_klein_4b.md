Notes: the best commercial-friendly local default candidate. It can take identity and pose/reference images in a unified editing workflow and is supported by ComfyUI and Diffusers. Multi-reference editing is not the same as deterministic skeleton control, so exact pose adherence needs product evaluation or a second ControlNet workflow.
